{推动浪简化版（实验）：用 ZIG 百分比折线代替原版的 ATR 折线，只检查 1-5 的硬规则，不画推演和数浪标签。需要函数库里有 ZIG、PEAK、TROUGH、PEAKBARS、TROUGHBARS、DRAWNULL。ZIG 会用到后面的K线，历史上的标记偏准，只有最新一根可信。两处 5 = 转折幅度 5%，可以一起改}
ZZ:ZIG(3,5),COLORYELLOW;
UPNOW:=TROUGHBARS(3,5,1)<PEAKBARS(3,5,1);
A0:=PEAK(3,5,3);
A1:=TROUGH(3,5,3);
A2:=PEAK(3,5,2);
A3:=TROUGH(3,5,2);
A4:=PEAK(3,5,1);
A5:=TROUGH(3,5,1);
DOWN5:=UPNOW AND A2<A0 AND A3<A1 AND A4<A1 AND A5<A3 AND NOT(A2-A3<A0-A1 AND A2-A3<A4-A5);
B0:=TROUGH(3,5,3);
B1:=PEAK(3,5,3);
B2:=TROUGH(3,5,2);
B3:=PEAK(3,5,2);
B4:=TROUGH(3,5,1);
B5:=PEAK(3,5,1);
UP5:=NOT(UPNOW) AND B2>B0 AND B3>B1 AND B4>B1 AND B5>B3 AND NOT(B3-B2<B1-B0 AND B3-B2<B5-B4);
TGT:IF(DOWN5,A5+(A0-A5)*0.5,DRAWNULL),COLORGREEN;
STP:IF(DOWN5,A5,DRAWNULL),COLORRED;
DRAWTEXT(DOWN5 AND NOT(REF(DOWN5,1)),LOW*0.97,'跌完五浪'),COLORGREEN;
DRAWTEXT(UP5 AND NOT(REF(UP5,1)),HIGH*1.03,'涨完五浪'),COLORRED;
